Welcome

Thank you for helping. You will check how well four different translations into render medical exam questions written in English.

  • 45 blocks, one exam question each, with four translations. About 2 to 3 hours in total.
  • You can stop at any time. Your work is saved automatically in this browser and is here when you come back on the same device and browser.
  • When you finish (or want a backup) press Export. It downloads a file; send that file to the person who invited you. Nothing is sent over the internet by this page.
  • To move to another device, export here and use More → Resume from a file there.

About you

Reported in the paper only in summary form (for example, "three reviewers with medical training"). Please do not enter your name or email in the code field unless asked.
